An interactive e commerce sales dashboard analyzing sales, profit, product performance, regional contribution, and sales trends to generate actionable business insights.

OBJECTIVES

1 Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
2 Identify top performing products and categories
3 Identify trends and areas for business improvement
4 Understand customer purchasing patterns

KEY INSIGHTS
-$10.07M in total sales
-$1.34M in total profit
-17,201 total units sold
-Monitors monthly sales trends across multiple years
-Identifies top performing products and regional sales contribution
